## The monthly report

One PDF, six sections. Direct MCP invocations, agent fetches of your pages, agent-attributed click-throughs, citation share across AI engines, conversion attribution where applicable, and three specific recommendations for the next month. You'll see exactly which AI engines surface your site, which prompts cite you, and how that ties to measurable business outcomes.
